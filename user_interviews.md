# User Interviews

IMPORTANT:
Only include REAL conversations and feedback.

Do NOT fabricate interviews.

---

## Recommended Structure

For each interview include:

* participant role
* team size
* current AI tooling usage
* biggest pain points
* surprising insights
* changes influenced by feedback

---

## Example Structure

### Interview 1

Role:
Startup founder

Team Size:
5

Current Tools:
ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor

Pain Points:

* overlapping subscriptions
* unclear ROI from multiple AI tools
* difficult budgeting visibility

Key Insight:
The founder cared more about removing redundant subscriptions than maximizing advanced optimization.

Product Changes Influenced:

* improved redundancy recommendation clarity
* emphasized conservative savings estimates

---

## Reflection

The interviews should influence:

* recommendation clarity
* landing page messaging
* report usability
* pricing trustworthiness

Real feedback is more valuable than artificially polished responses.
