# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target Users

Primary audience:

* startup founders
* small engineering teams
* AI-heavy product teams
* cost-conscious SaaS startups

---

## Core Problem

Many startups accumulate overlapping AI subscriptions without:

* clear visibility
* pricing awareness
* optimization discipline

This creates:

* redundant spend
* overlapping tooling
* unnecessary premium subscriptions

---

## Positioning

SpendLayer positions itself as:

* a deterministic AI spend auditor
* a practical optimization tool
* a conservative recommendation system

The product intentionally avoids:

* hype-driven AI automation
* speculative ROI promises
* black-box recommendation systems

---

## Acquisition Channels

Potential acquisition channels:

* LinkedIn founder content
* startup communities
* Reddit productivity discussions
* indie hacker communities
* developer Twitter/X audiences

---

## Distribution Strategy

Initial strategy:

* lightweight landing page
* shareable reports
* founder-focused messaging
* organic content marketing

Longer-term:

* partnerships with startup operators
* AI tooling comparison content
* budgeting-focused SaaS communities

---

## Competitive Advantage

SpendLayer differentiates itself through:

* deterministic recommendations
* explainable pricing logic
* conservative savings philosophy
* lightweight UX
* startup-focused workflows
