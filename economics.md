# Economics

## Assumptions

SpendLayer is modeled as a lightweight founder-led SaaS with zero paid acquisition during the first validation phase.

All numbers are conservative planning estimates, not claimed traction.

## CAC

Early CAC should be mostly founder time.

Estimated zero-budget CAC:

- Direct outreach: $0 cash, 15-25 minutes per qualified user
- Community posts: $0 cash, 2-4 hours per useful post
- Product Hunt / Hacker News launch: $0 cash, unpredictable volume
- Referral through shared reports: $0 cash, depends on report usefulness

Planning CAC once paid experiments begin:

- Founder-led organic: $0-$25 cash CAC
- Content and SEO: $25-$75 blended CAC after compounding
- Small paid tests: $100-$250 CAC until proven otherwise

Paid acquisition should not start until activation and lead quality are proven.

## Funnel Assumptions

Visitor to audit start:

- Conservative: 15%
- Base case: 25%
- Strong: 35%

Audit start to completed report:

- Conservative: 40%
- Base case: 55%
- Strong: 70%

Completed report to captured lead:

- Conservative: 5%
- Base case: 10%
- Strong: 15%

Captured lead to consultation request:

- Conservative: 5%
- Base case: 10%
- Strong: 15%

Consultation request to paid customer:

- Conservative: 10%
- Base case: 20%
- Strong: 30%

## Contract Values

Potential packaging:

- Self-serve monitoring: $19-$49/month
- Startup team plan: $99-$199/month
- One-time AI spend review: $250-$750
- Lightweight advisory retainer: $500-$1,500/month

Conservative average contract values:

- Self-serve ACV: $300/year
- Team plan ACV: $1,200/year
- Advisory ACV: $3,000/year

No enterprise ACV is assumed for the assignment because the product does not yet support enterprise procurement workflows.

## ARR Pathway Math

Path to $1K ARR:

- 4 self-serve customers at $25/month
- or 1 team customer at roughly $99/month

Path to $10K ARR:

- 34 self-serve customers at $25/month
- or 9 team customers at $99/month
- or 4 advisory customers at $250/month

Path to $50K ARR:

- 167 self-serve customers at $25/month
- or 43 team customers at $99/month
- or 17 advisory customers at $250/month

The most realistic early path is services-assisted SaaS: use free audits to identify high-intent founders, convert a small number into paid reviews, then learn which recurring monitoring features are worth building.

## Unit Economics Guardrails

- Keep report generation deterministic and cheap.
- Avoid expensive AI calls in the critical path.
- Use AI summaries only as optional narrative polish.
- Do not build dashboards, auth, or billing until there is evidence of repeat usage or paid consultation demand.
