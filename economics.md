# Economics

## Product Economics Philosophy

SpendLayer is designed as a lightweight SaaS MVP with minimal infrastructure complexity.

The system intentionally prioritizes:

* low operational overhead
* deterministic computation
* lightweight persistence
* scalable frontend rendering

---

## Infrastructure Costs

Primary infrastructure:

* Vercel hosting
* Supabase persistence
* optional AI summary generation

Estimated early-stage costs are expected to remain low because:

* recommendation logic is synchronous
* reports are lightweight JSON payloads
* pricing calculations are deterministic

---

## Potential Monetization

Possible monetization models:

* premium audit exports
* team dashboards
* recurring audit monitoring
* advanced reporting
* procurement optimization tooling

---

## Customer Value Proposition

The product aims to provide value through:

* spend visibility
* subscription optimization
* tooling consolidation
* pricing awareness

Even small monthly savings can justify product usage for startup teams managing multiple AI subscriptions.

---

## Cost Constraints

The architecture intentionally avoids:

* GPU-heavy workloads
* expensive AI orchestration
* real-time infrastructure
* excessive backend processing

This keeps infrastructure economics predictable and scalable.
